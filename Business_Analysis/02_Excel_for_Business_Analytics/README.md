# Module 02 — Excel for Business Analytics

## 1. Overview

Excel is a practical tool for transforming business data into reliable, actionable information. This module develops the skills needed to organize datasets, clean records, calculate business KPIs, summarize performance, build dashboards, and evaluate business scenarios.

The emphasis is on analytical reasoning rather than memorizing formulas. Every analysis should answer a business question, use a clearly defined metric, validate its calculations, and communicate its limitations.

## 2. Prerequisites

* Basic computer literacy and file management.
* Familiarity with percentages, averages, and arithmetic.
* Recommended: completion of Module 01 — Business Analytics Fundamentals.
* No previous Excel experience is required.

## 3. Learning objectives

By the end of this module, learners will be able to:

1. Design structured, auditable Excel workbooks.
2. Import, clean, validate, and organize business data.
3. Use relative, absolute, and mixed cell references correctly.
4. Apply IF, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS, IFERROR, and lookup functions.
5. Calculate revenue, gross profit, gross margin, average order value, and operational metrics.
6. Build PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and conditional formatting rules.
7. Select visualizations appropriate to business questions.
8. Perform sensitivity analysis and What-If Analysis.
9. Identify common spreadsheet errors and implement quality controls.
10. Produce management-ready reports and evidence-based recommendations.

The learner should produce:

## 11. Professional standard

A successful submission must be reproducible, internally consistent, clearly documented, and appropriate for a business stakeholder. Do not claim that a metric proves causation when the analysis only establishes an association. Report assumptions and limitations alongside the results.
