# Business Analytics

A course-style repository covering business analytics from fundamentals to advanced methods and business applications.

## Course Modules
1. Business Analytics Fundamentals
2. Excel for Business Analytics
3. Statistics and Probability
4. SQL for Business Analytics
5. Python for Business Analytics
6. Data Collection and Preparation
7. Exploratory Data Analysis
8. KPIs and Performance Management
9. Data Visualization
10. Business Intelligence and Dashboards
11. Hypothesis Testing and Inference
12. Regression and Predictive Analytics
13. Time Series and Forecasting
14. Optimization and Operations Research
15. Experimental Design and A/B Testing
16. Customer and Marketing Analytics
17. Financial and Risk Analytics
18. Supply Chain and Operations Analytics
19. Causal Analytics and Decision Science
20. Communicating Business Insights
21. Capstone Projects

## Workflow
Learn concepts, practice examples, complete exercises, apply methods to a business case, and communicate actionable recommendations.

## Tools
Excel, SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter, Power BI, Tableau, Git.

## Data Policy
Use public, synthetic, or appropriately licensed datasets. Never commit confidential or personally identifiable data.
