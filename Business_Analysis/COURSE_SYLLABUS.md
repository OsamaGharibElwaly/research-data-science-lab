# Business Analytics Course Syllabus

## Level 1 — Foundations
Modules 01–05: Business fundamentals, Excel, statistics, SQL, Python.

## Level 2 — Analytical Practice
Modules 06–10: Data preparation, EDA, KPIs, visualization, dashboards.

## Level 3 — Advanced Analytics
Modules 11–15: Inference, predictive analytics, forecasting, optimization, experiments.

## Level 4 — Business Specializations
Modules 16–20: Marketing, finance, supply chain, causal analysis, communication.

## Level 5 — Capstone
Module 21: Retail, marketing, forecasting, and supply chain projects.

Standard workflow: Theory → Examples → Exercises → Case Study → Recommendations.
