## 6. Follow-up Rules

* Be respectful of the reference's time.
* Send only relevant and accurate experience information.
* Never ask a reference to confirm activities they did not observe or cannot reasonably verify.
* Do not pressure anyone to provide an inaccurate endorsement.
* Follow the official INCOSE process instead of relying solely on informal letters.
* Maintain a backup list in case a potential reference is unavailable.
* Protect private contact details and confidential project information.
