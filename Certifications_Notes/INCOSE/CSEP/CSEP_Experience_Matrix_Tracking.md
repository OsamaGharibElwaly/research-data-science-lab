**Rules for maintaining this inventory**

1. Record dates using `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Distinguish employment duration from time spent performing qualifying systems engineering work.
3. Do not count the same calendar period twice merely because two projects overlapped.
4. Record part-time work and changes in responsibilities accurately.
5. Do not treat coursework or personal study as professional experience unless current INCOSE rules explicitly permit the activity to count.
6. Keep confidential evidence in an approved private location and reference it by identifier here.

## 7. Quality Review

For every experience entry, ask:

* [ ] Are the dates accurate and consistent with records?
* [ ] Is the system context understandable?
* [ ] Are personal responsibilities explicit?
* [ ] Are the activities genuinely systems engineering activities?
* [ ] Is each mapped area justified by actual work?
* [ ] Is the evidence available and permissible to share?
* [ ] Can a suitable reference independently confirm the experience?
* [ ] Are outcomes factual and proportionate?
* [ ] Have overlapping periods been handled correctly?
* [ ] Have current INCOSE requirements been checked?
