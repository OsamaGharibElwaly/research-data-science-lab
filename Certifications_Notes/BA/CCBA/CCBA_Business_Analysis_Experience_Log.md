# CCBA Business Analysis Experience Log

## 1. Purpose

This log organizes professional activities that may be relevant to CCBA eligibility and helps document practical business analysis experience.

It is a personal evidence tracker. It does not establish that an activity qualifies under IIBA rules.

Before counting hours or submitting an entry, verify the current CCBA experience requirements and definitions on the official IIBA website.

* [ ] Is the activity described clearly?
* [ ] Is the date range accurate?

* [ ] Is the activity genuinely related to business analysis?
* [ ] Is the relationship to the relevant knowledge area clear?

* [ ] Have confidential organizational details been protected?

## 8. Distinguishing Business Analysis from Related Work

Some activities may overlap with business analysis but are not automatically qualifying.

Examples that may be relevant depending on actual responsibilities and current eligibility rules:

* Eliciting and documenting business needs.
* Analyzing business processes.
* Developing and validating requirements.
* Assessing solution alternatives.
* Facilitating stakeholder decisions.
* Evaluating solution performance.
* Managing requirement changes.
* Defining future-state capabilities.

Activities that should not automatically be counted without checking their relevance include:

* Routine software coding without business analysis responsibilities.
* General IT support.
* Pure administrative work.
* Unrelated project coordination.
* Self-study without qualifying professional experience.
* Academic coursework, unless the applicable rules explicitly recognize it.

The activity performed matters more than the job title alone.
