# CCBA Professional Development Tracking

## 1. Purpose

This document tracks professional learning activities that may be relevant to CCBA application preparation and long-term business analysis development.

It helps organize course details, completion evidence, hours, and subject coverage.

**Important:** Not every course, certificate, webinar, or self-study activity automatically qualifies toward IIBA professional development requirements. Confirm the current CCBA rules and accepted activity definitions before claiming hours.

Official starting point: https://www.iiba.org/

## 6. Suggested Development Activities

Select activities based on learning needs and verify eligibility separately.

### Formal Courses

Possible subjects:

* Business analysis fundamentals.
* Requirements engineering.
* Process modeling.
* Business process management.
* Stakeholder engagement.
* Business strategy.
* Data analysis for business decisions.
* Agile business analysis.
* Solution evaluation.

### Practical Exercises

* Model an existing business process.
* Conduct a simulated stakeholder interview.
* Write testable requirements.
* Create a traceability matrix.
* Prepare a business case.
* Compare strategic alternatives.
* Define KPIs and a measurement plan.
* Conduct a mock requirements review.

Practical exercises can strengthen capability even when their eligibility as formal professional development hours is uncertain or not recognized.

### Professional Reading

Possible resources include:

* The current BABOK Guide.
* Official IIBA guidance.
* Business process management references.
* Requirements engineering literature.
* Agile analysis resources.
* Professional ethics guidance.

Record actual time and activities honestly. Do not claim the same learning time multiple times.

## 7. Evidence Management

For each activity, retain suitable evidence such as:

* Completion certificates.
* Provider transcripts.
* Attendance records.
* Course descriptions.
* Learning objectives.
* Documented course duration.
* Assessment results, if available.
* Provider confirmation.

Store records securely and maintain enough information to verify the activity later.

## 8. Monthly Review

At the end of each month:

1. Update completed activities.
2. Record actual hours.
3. Attach or reference evidence.
4. Verify activity eligibility where possible.
5. Update knowledge-area coverage.
6. Identify gaps.
7. Schedule the next learning activity.
