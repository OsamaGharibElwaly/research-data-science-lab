# CCBA Weak Areas and Revision Log

## 1. Purpose

This log helps identify, prioritize, and improve weak areas during CCBA preparation.

The objective is not merely to record incorrect answers. It is to understand why errors occur, strengthen the underlying concept, and verify improvement through later practice.

## 6. Common Conceptual Confusions

Use this table to track concepts that are easy to confuse.

| Concept pair                                     | Key distinction                                                                                | Self-assessment |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Need vs. solution                                | Need explains why change is required; solution is one possible response                        | [Strong/Review] |
| Requirement vs. design                           | Requirement expresses a need or condition; design describes a possible solution representation | [Strong/Review] |
| Verification vs. validation                      | Verification checks requirement quality; validation checks alignment with actual needs         | [Strong/Review] |
| Current state vs. future state                   | Current state describes today; future state describes the desired condition                    | [Strong/Review] |
| Functional vs. non-functional requirement        | Behavior vs. quality attribute or operating condition                                          | [Strong/Review] |
| Business requirement vs. stakeholder requirement | Organizational outcome vs. a stakeholder's need                                                | [Strong/Review] |
| Risk vs. issue                                   | Risk is uncertain; an issue is a condition or event that already exists                        | [Strong/Review] |
| Traceability vs. prioritization                  | Relationships among artifacts vs. relative importance and sequencing                           | [Strong/Review] |
| Solution performance vs. business value          | How the solution operates vs. the value it creates                                             | [Strong/Review] |
| Approval vs. validation                          | Authorization to proceed vs. confirmation that requirements represent needs                    | [Strong/Review] |

## 9. Weekly Revision Plan

### Week 1 — Core Concepts

* Review business needs, stakeholders, requirements, designs, and value.
* Review the six knowledge areas.
* Complete a short practice set.
* Record conceptual confusions.

### Week 2 — Planning and Elicitation

* Review analysis planning and stakeholder engagement.
* Practice interviews and workshop scenarios.
* Revise communication and collaboration.
* Retest previously incorrect questions.

### Week 3 — Requirements and Strategy

* Review traceability and change assessment.
* Practice current-state and future-state analysis.
* Write measurable requirements.
* Practice prioritization and impact analysis.

### Week 4 — Solution Evaluation and Ethics

* Review KPIs, baselines, and benefits realization.
* Practice underperformance scenarios.
* Review confidentiality, integrity, and conflicts of interest.
* Complete a mixed mock exam.

Adapt the schedule to the available study time and the results of practice assessments.

## 10. Rules for Closing a Weak Area

Do not mark a topic as resolved merely because you reread it.

A topic can be marked resolved when:

1. You can explain the concept in your own words.
2. You can distinguish it from related concepts.
3. You can apply it to a new scenario.
4. You answer relevant practice questions correctly.
5. You can explain why the incorrect options are weaker.
6. You retain the understanding in a later review.
