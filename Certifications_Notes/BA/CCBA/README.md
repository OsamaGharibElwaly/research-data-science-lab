# IIBA CCBA Certification Study Repository

## 1. Overview

This repository contains structured study materials for preparing for the **IIBA Certification of Capability in Business Analysis (CCBA)**.

The materials are organized as Markdown documents covering business analysis concepts, knowledge areas, requirements management, exam revision, practice questions, application tracking, experience records, professional development, and weak-area analysis.

The repository is intended for personal learning and preparation. It is not an official IIBA publication and does not guarantee examination success or application eligibility.

## 3. Recommended Study Order

### Phase 1 — Foundations

Study the initial modules to establish the terminology, purpose, and core principles of business analysis.

Focus on:

* Business needs and value.
* Stakeholders.
* Requirements and designs.
* Business analysis approaches.
* Planning and monitoring.
* Elicitation and collaboration.

### Phase 2 — Applied Business Analysis

Study the intermediate modules and practice applying concepts to realistic business situations.

Focus on:

* Requirements life cycle management.
* Strategy analysis.
* Requirements analysis and design.
* Solution evaluation.
* Business intelligence and analytics.
* Technology and organizational perspectives.

### Phase 3 — Professional Competence

Review professional behavior, analytical thinking, collaboration, and ethical responsibilities.

Focus on:

* Critical thinking.
* Systems thinking.
* Problem-solving.
* Communication.
* Professional integrity.
* Confidentiality.
* Conflicts of interest.
* Continuous development.

### Phase 4 — Exam Preparation

Use the revision guide and practice questions to assess understanding.

1. Complete a practice set without consulting the answers.
2. Review the explanations.
3. Record mistakes in `CCBA_Weak_Areas_and_Revision_Log.md`.
4. Revisit the corresponding study modules.
5. Repeat the questions later.
6. Confirm all official examination requirements before booking.

## 4. Application Preparation

Use the tracking documents to organize potential eligibility evidence.

### Application and Eligibility

`CCBA_Application_and_Eligibility_Tracking.md`

Track current eligibility requirements, application progress, reference status if applicable, and outstanding items.

### Business Analysis Experience

`CCBA_Business_Analysis_Experience_Log.md`

Record actual responsibilities, dates, hours, deliverables, knowledge areas, and supporting evidence.

### Professional Development

`CCBA_Professional_Development_Tracking.md`

Record courses and learning activities, including their dates, duration, evidence, and eligibility status.

### Weak Areas

`CCBA_Weak_Areas_and_Revision_Log.md`

Track errors, revision sessions, practice scores, and improvement actions.

These documents help organize preparation but do not determine whether IIBA will accept an application. Verify all eligibility details against current official requirements.

## 5. Standard Module Format

Where practical, study modules should contain:

1. Learning objectives.
2. Concept explanations.
3. Definitions and distinctions.
4. Techniques and frameworks.
5. Examples.
6. Practical case studies.
7. Knowledge-check questions.
8. Answer explanations.
9. Revision summaries.
10. Further study recommendations.

Use consistent terminology and avoid treating illustrative examples as official examination questions.

## 7. Recommended Study Workflow

For each module:

1. Read the module carefully.
2. Summarize key concepts in your own words.
3. Create examples from a real or hypothetical business.
4. Answer knowledge-check questions without looking at the key.
5. Record incorrect answers and their underlying causes.
6. Revisit weak topics.
7. Review the module again after a delay.
8. Update the progress tracker.

Focus on reasoning and application rather than memorizing definitions alone.
