# CCBA Application and Eligibility Tracking

## 1. Purpose

This document tracks preparation for applying to the IIBA Certification of Capability in Business Analysis (CCBA).

It is a personal planning and evidence-management document, not an official eligibility determination or application form.

**Important:** IIBA can change eligibility criteria, fees, documentation requirements, application procedures, and exam policies. Verify the current requirements through the official IIBA website before submitting an application.

Official starting point: https://www.iiba.org/
