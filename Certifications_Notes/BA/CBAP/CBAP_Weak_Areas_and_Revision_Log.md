## 9. Revision Techniques

### Active Recall

Close the study material and explain a concept from memory.

### Spaced Repetition

Revisit difficult concepts at increasing intervals.

### Interleaved Practice

Mix question types and knowledge areas to improve the ability to identify the relevant concept in unfamiliar scenarios.

### Error Review

Prioritize recurring reasoning errors instead of simply repeating questions already answered correctly.

### Teach-Back

Explain a concept to another person or write a short explanation without copying the source material.
