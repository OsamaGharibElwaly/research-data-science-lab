# CBAP Certification Notes

## Overview

This repository contains structured Markdown study notes and tracking templates for preparation toward the Certified Business Analysis Professional (CBAP) credential from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).

The materials are designed to support:

* Business analysis concept revision.
* BABOK knowledge-area study.
* Scenario-based practice.
* Exam preparation.
* Experience and professional development recordkeeping.
* Application administration.
* Weak-area identification and continuous improvement.

These files are independent study resources. They are not official IIBA publications, examination questions, application forms, or a guarantee of eligibility or certification.

## Recommended Study Workflow

### Phase 1: Foundations

Study Modules 01–04. Establish a strong understanding of business analysis fundamentals, planning, elicitation, and collaboration.

### Phase 2: Requirements and Strategy

Study Modules 05–08. Focus on requirements traceability, change management, strategy analysis, requirement quality, design options, and solution evaluation.

### Phase 3: Applied Techniques

Study Modules 09–16. Practice advanced analysis, process improvement, adaptive delivery, analytics, risk assessment, and value evaluation.

### Phase 4: Leadership and Professional Practice

Study Modules 17–18. Review strategic alignment, governance, leadership, professional competencies, and ethical judgment.

### Phase 5: Examination Preparation

Study Modules 19–20. Review key distinctions, solve scenario questions, analyze incorrect answers, and maintain the weak-area tracker.

### Phase 6: Application Readiness

Use the tracking documents to organize experience evidence, professional development records, references, and administrative tasks.

## Recommended Study Session

1. Read one module.
2. Summarize the key concepts from memory.
3. Explain important distinctions in your own words.
4. Complete the practice questions without viewing the answers first.
5. Review every incorrect or uncertain response.
6. Record weak areas.
7. Revisit difficult concepts after a delay.
8. Apply the concepts to a realistic business scenario.

## File Naming Convention

Study materials use numbered Markdown filenames to preserve a logical order.

Tracking files use descriptive names without numerical prefixes because they support multiple study phases.

## Updating the Notes

When revising a file:

* Keep the Markdown structure consistent.
* Use practical examples.
* Distinguish assumptions from established facts.
* Include explanations for practice answers.
* Record the source and date for changing certification requirements.
* Avoid presenting original practice questions as official IIBA exam content.
* Review overlapping topics for consistency.
