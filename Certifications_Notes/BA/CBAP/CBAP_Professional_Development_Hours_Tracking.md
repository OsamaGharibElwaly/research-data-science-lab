# CBAP Professional Development Hours Tracking

## 1. Purpose

Use this tracker to record professional development activities that may qualify for CBAP application or maintenance purposes, as applicable.

**Important:** Verify the current IIBA requirements for the relevant credential and application stage. Do not assume that all self-study, online videos, courses, or certifications qualify.

## 6. Calculating Eligible Hours

Maintain three separate totals:

1. **Raw learning hours:** Total recorded time across activities.
2. **Potentially eligible hours:** Time that appears relevant but has not been fully verified.
3. **Verified eligible hours:** Time confirmed against the current official requirements.

Do not present raw hours as verified hours.

Use a spreadsheet or a calculator to total only entries that meet the applicable rules.

## 7. Evidence Checklist

For each activity:

* [ ] Date completed recorded.
* [ ] Duration documented.
* [ ] Learning objectives recorded.

* [ ] Duplicate hours checked.
* [ ] Eligibility reviewed against current official guidance.
* [ ] Entry included in the verified-hours total only when justified.

## 8. Monthly Review

At each review:

1. Add completed activities.
2. Verify recorded duration.
3. Save supporting evidence.
4. Review eligibility.
5. Recalculate raw and verified totals.
6. Identify any remaining gaps.
7. Record the date and reviewer.
