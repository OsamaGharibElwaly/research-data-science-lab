At the end of each month or engagement:

## 11. Important Notes

* Be truthful and precise.
* Do not exaggerate responsibilities.
* Keep evidence confidential and secure.
* Do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information.
* Verify current eligibility requirements before calculating totals.
* Retain records in case the application requires clarification.

This log is a personal recordkeeping tool, not an official IIBA form or an eligibility determination.
