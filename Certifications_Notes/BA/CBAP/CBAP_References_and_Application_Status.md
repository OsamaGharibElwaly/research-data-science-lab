## 5. Reference Preparation Checklist

For each potential reference:

* [ ] Confirm that the relationship is acceptable under current IIBA rules.
* [ ] Confirm that the person can verify relevant professional experience.
* [ ] Explain the purpose of the reference request.
* [ ] Confirm willingness and availability.
* [ ] Provide accurate context about the engagement.
* [ ] Follow the official submission process.
* [ ] Record completion and any follow-up needed.

Do not ask a reference to verify work they did not observe or cannot reasonably confirm.
