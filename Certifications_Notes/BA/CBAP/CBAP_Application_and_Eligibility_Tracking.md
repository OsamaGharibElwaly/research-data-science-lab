## 4. Business Analysis Experience Evidence

For each relevant engagement, record:

* Employer or client.
* Role and dates.
* Project or initiative.
* Business need.
* Work performed.
* Relevant business analysis tasks.
* Outcomes and evidence.
* Reference who can verify the experience.

Use the separate `CBAP_Business_Analysis_Experience_Log.md` for detailed records.

### Experience Evidence Checklist

* [ ] Employment dates verified.
* [ ] Relevant project dates documented.
* [ ] Responsibilities described accurately.
* [ ] Business analysis activities identified.
* [ ] Work examples prepared.
* [ ] Outcomes and evidence recorded.
* [ ] Overlapping periods checked to avoid double counting where prohibited.
* [ ] Experience reviewed against the current IIBA criteria.

## 5. Professional Development Evidence

Use `CBAP_Professional_Development_Hours_Tracking.md` to record courses, workshops, training, and other potentially eligible learning activities.

For each activity, record:

* Provider.
* Course or activity title.
* Date.
* Duration.
* Learning objectives.
* Relevant content.
* Supporting evidence.
* Eligibility verified against current IIBA guidance.

Do not assume every course, video, self-study session, or certification automatically qualifies. Verify eligibility and acceptable documentation.

## 7. Application Workflow

### Stage 1: Research

* [ ] Open the official IIBA CBAP page.
* [ ] Review current eligibility requirements.
* [ ] Review application instructions.
* [ ] Record the date and source of verification.

### Stage 2: Prepare Evidence

* [ ] Complete the experience log.
* [ ] Review professional development evidence.
* [ ] Contact references.
* [ ] Prepare accurate project descriptions.
* [ ] Resolve gaps or inconsistencies.

### Stage 3: Submit

* [ ] Complete the application.
* [ ] Review all entries for accuracy.
* [ ] Submit supporting information as required.
* [ ] Pay applicable fees through the official process.
* [ ] Save the confirmation and application reference securely.

### Stage 4: Follow Up

* [ ] Monitor application status.
* [ ] Respond to requests for clarification.
* [ ] Record the decision and any required actions.
* [ ] Schedule the examination only after confirming applicable authorization and timing rules.
