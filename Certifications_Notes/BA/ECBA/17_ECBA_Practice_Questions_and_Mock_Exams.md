## 3. Essential Concepts to Review

### Business Analysis

Business analysis enables change by defining needs and recommending solutions that deliver value to stakeholders in a context.

### Core Terms

* **Need:** A problem or opportunity to address.
* **Change:** The transformation of an organization in response to a need.
* **Solution:** A specific way of satisfying one or more needs.
* **Stakeholder:** A person, group, or organization that relates to a change, need, or solution.
* **Value:** The worth, importance, or usefulness of something to a stakeholder in a context.
* **Requirement:** A usable representation of a need.
* **Design:** A representation of a solution.
* **Business requirement:** A goal, objective, or outcome that describes why a change is needed.
* **Stakeholder requirement:** A need of a stakeholder or stakeholder group.
* **Solution requirement:** A capability or quality that a solution must have to meet stakeholder requirements.
* **Transition requirement:** A temporary capability needed to move from the current state to a desired future state.

## 4. Practice Questions

### Question 1: Business Analysis Purpose

An organization experiences increasing customer complaints because its order-processing system is slow. What should the business analyst do first?

A. Purchase a faster server.

B. Investigate the problem, understand the business need, and establish the current state.

C. Replace the entire system.

D. Train all employees on the existing system.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** The analyst must understand the need and current state before recommending a solution. The other options prematurely assume a cause or solution.

### Question 2: Stakeholder Identification

A new inventory system will affect warehouse staff, purchasing employees, suppliers, and finance. What is the best action?

A. Interview only the project sponsor.

B. Identify relevant stakeholders and analyze their roles, interests, and influence.

C. Invite every stakeholder to every meeting.

D. Allow the development team to determine stakeholder needs.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Stakeholder analysis identifies people affected by or able to influence the change. Engagement should then be tailored to their needs and responsibilities.

### Question 3: Requirement Classification

"The organization wants to reduce order-processing time by 30% within six months."

What type of requirement is this most likely to represent?

A. Transition requirement.

B. Solution requirement.

C. Business requirement.

D. Interface requirement.

**Correct answer: C**

**Explanation:** It states a business outcome and measurable objective rather than a specific system capability.

### Question 4: Elicitation

A stakeholder says, "The system is difficult to use." What should the analyst do?

A. Record the statement as a complete functional requirement.

B. Ask targeted questions and investigate specific usability problems.

C. Ignore the comment because it is subjective.

D. Immediately redesign the interface.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** The statement is vague. Follow-up questions and observation can reveal concrete problems and requirements.

### Question 5: Requirements Validation

A requirement is clearly written and approved by the stakeholder, but it does not contribute to the business objective. What is the main concern?

A. The requirement lacks value or alignment with the need.

B. The requirement must be functional.

C. The requirement is automatically invalid because it is approved.

D. The requirement must be a transition requirement.

**Correct answer: A**

**Explanation:** Approval and clarity do not guarantee that a requirement delivers value. Requirements should support the need and intended outcomes.

### Question 6: Requirements Verification

An analyst checks whether a requirement is unambiguous, complete, consistent, and testable. Which activity is being performed?

A. Requirements verification.

B. Solution implementation.

C. Benefits realization.

D. Stakeholder identification.

**Correct answer: A**

**Explanation:** Verification examines the quality of the requirement itself. Validation examines whether it supports the need and expected value.

### Question 7: Traceability

A developer asks why a particular feature exists. Which artifact is most useful for showing its relationship to the original business objective?

A. Meeting attendance sheet.

B. Requirements traceability information.

C. Employee directory.

D. Project calendar alone.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Traceability connects requirements to their sources, objectives, related requirements, designs, and tests as appropriate.

### Question 8: Process Modeling

A business analyst wants to understand how an order moves from submission through payment and delivery. Which technique is most appropriate?

A. Stakeholder register only.

B. Process modeling.

C. Financial forecasting only.

D. Brainstorming without recording outcomes.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Process models represent activities, decisions, flows, and responsibilities.

### Question 9: Change Control

After requirements approval, a stakeholder requests a new feature that may affect cost and schedule. What should the analyst do?

A. Accept it immediately.

B. Reject all changes automatically.

C. Assess the change, its impact, value, dependencies, and approval needs.

D. Ask the developer to add it without informing anyone.

**Correct answer: C**

**Explanation:** Changes should be evaluated according to the project's governance and requirements management approach.

### Question 10: Communication

A technical team and business stakeholders repeatedly misunderstand one another. What is the best response?

A. Use technical terminology with everyone.

B. Tailor communication methods, terminology, and detail to each audience.

C. Stop stakeholder meetings.

D. Send the same lengthy technical document to everyone.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Effective communication considers audience, purpose, context, and preferred communication method.

### Question 11: Solution Evaluation

A new system has been implemented, but customer satisfaction has not improved. What should the analyst investigate?

A. Whether the solution delivers the expected value and addresses the original need.

B. Whether the project documents have attractive formatting.

C. Whether every stakeholder attended the launch.

D. Whether requirements can be deleted.

**Correct answer: A**

**Explanation:** Solution evaluation compares actual performance and outcomes with expected value, considering solution limitations and external factors.

### Question 12: Prioritization

A team has limited resources and cannot implement every requested feature in the first release. What should guide prioritization?

A. The loudest stakeholder's opinion alone.

B. Business value, risk, dependencies, urgency, and stakeholder needs.

C. Alphabetical order.

D. The developer's personal preference alone.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Prioritization balances value and constraints using an agreed approach.

### Question 13: Functional Requirement

Which statement is a functional requirement?

A. The system should be easy to use.

B. The system must be reliable.

C. The system shall allow authorized users to cancel an unshipped order.

D. The project should reduce operational costs.

**Correct answer: C**

**Explanation:** It specifies a behavior or capability that the solution must provide.

### Question 14: Non-Functional Requirement

Which statement is a non-functional requirement?

A. The system shall create an invoice.

B. The system shall allow a user to reset a password.

C. The system shall process 95% of standard searches within two seconds under the specified load.

D. The system shall approve a valid purchase order.

**Correct answer: C**

**Explanation:** It specifies a measurable performance characteristic. The other options primarily describe behaviors.

### Question 15: Root Cause

Employees report that they frequently enter incorrect product quantities. What is the best analytical approach?

A. Assume employees need punishment.

B. Investigate the process, interface, training, data quality, and underlying causes.

C. Replace every employee.

D. Immediately automate the entire process.

**Correct answer: B**

**Explanation:** Symptoms do not establish root causes. Investigate evidence before selecting an intervention.

   * A. To remove all stakeholder involvement.
   * B. To determine relative importance under constraints.
   * C. To avoid documenting requirements.
   * D. To ensure all requirements are implemented simultaneously.

6. What is the purpose of a glossary?

   * A. To establish consistent meanings for important terms.
   * B. To record every meeting.
   * C. To replace all process models.
   * D. To approve solution deployment.

7. Which is an example of a transition requirement?

   * A. The system shall calculate tax.
   * B. The organization needs to reduce processing costs.
   * C. Existing customer records must be migrated before launch.
   * D. The system shall support 1,000 concurrent users.

8. A stakeholder disagrees with a proposed requirement. What should the analyst do?

   * A. Ignore the disagreement.
   * B. Explore the concern, evidence, impacts, and decision authority.
   * C. Delete the requirement immediately.
   * D. Allow the development team to decide without consultation.

   * A. They clarify conditions that must be met for a requirement or deliverable to be accepted.
   * B. They eliminate the need for testing.
   * C. They replace business objectives.
   * D. They automatically prevent scope changes.

10. What should happen when an implemented solution fails to achieve expected results?

    * A. Declare success because deployment is complete.
    * B. Evaluate actual outcomes, investigate causes, and identify improvements.
    * C. Stop collecting data.
    * D. Delete the original business case.

### Answers: Mini Mock Exam A

1. **B** — Stakeholder analysis supports effective engagement.
2. **C** — It defines a measurable baseline, target, and time frame.
3. **A** — Observation reveals actual work practices.
4. **A** — Use cases describe actor-system interactions toward goals.
5. **B** — Prioritization supports decisions under constraints.
6. **A** — A glossary promotes shared understanding.
7. **C** — Data migration is a temporary capability needed for transition.
8. **B** — Disagreement should be explored and managed transparently.
9. **A** — Acceptance criteria define conditions for acceptance.
10. **B** — Evaluation compares outcomes with expectations and identifies causes.

## 7. Scenario-Based Practice

### Scenario: Retail Inventory Transformation

A retail organization uses spreadsheets to manage inventory. Stock discrepancies occur frequently, purchasing is delayed, and managers lack reliable reports.

#### Tasks

1. Identify the likely business need.
2. List five stakeholders.
3. Write two measurable business objectives.
4. Identify three elicitation techniques.
5. Write two functional requirements.
6. Write two non-functional requirements.
7. Identify one transition requirement.
8. Suggest criteria for prioritizing requirements.
9. Describe how the organization could evaluate the solution.

#### Model Answer

**Business need:** Improve inventory accuracy and availability while reducing manual effort and purchasing delays.

**Stakeholders:** Warehouse staff, purchasing staff, finance, store managers, suppliers, and IT support.

**Business objectives:**

* Reduce inventory discrepancy rate from the measured baseline by an agreed percentage within six months.
* Reduce average purchase-order processing time by 25% within six months.

Targets should be confirmed against baseline data and business feasibility.

**Elicitation techniques:** Interviews, observation, and document analysis.

**Functional requirements:**

* The system shall allow authorized staff to record stock receipts and issues.
* The system shall generate a low-stock alert when available inventory falls below an approved reorder threshold.

**Non-functional requirements:**

* The system shall meet an agreed availability target during defined business hours.
* Standard inventory searches shall complete within an agreed response-time target under specified load.

**Transition requirement:** Existing product and inventory records must be cleansed and migrated before go-live.

**Prioritization criteria:** Business value, risk reduction, urgency, dependencies, implementation effort, and regulatory needs.

**Evaluation:** Compare post-implementation discrepancy rates, processing times, stockout frequency, user feedback, and total operating costs against baseline measures.

## 8. Exam-Day Strategy

* Read the question stem carefully.
* Identify the business context and the exact problem being asked.
* Eliminate answers that assume a solution before understanding the need.
* Prefer evidence-based analysis, stakeholder engagement, and value alignment.
* Distinguish requirement quality from requirement relevance.
* Do not assume that the most technical answer is the best business answer.
* Review incorrect responses and identify the underlying knowledge gap.
* Follow the current official exam instructions for format, duration, and permitted materials.
