# ECBA Application and Eligibility Tracking

## 1. Purpose

This document tracks preparation and administrative steps for applying for the IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA).

It is a personal tracking template, not an official application form. Confirm current eligibility rules, application procedures, fees, exam policies, and certification requirements directly with IIBA.

## 8. Examination Scheduling Decision

Before scheduling, confirm:

* [ ] I have reviewed the current exam blueprint.
* [ ] I have covered the required learning topics.
* [ ] I have completed at least two timed mock exams.
* [ ] I understand my most common mistakes.
* [ ] My performance is consistently meeting my chosen practice target.
* [ ] I have a realistic revision plan.
* [ ] I have checked the official appointment and rescheduling policies.
* [ ] I can meet the examination date without compromising essential financial commitments.

The practice target is a personal study benchmark, not a guarantee of passing.
