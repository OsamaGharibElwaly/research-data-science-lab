### Score Calculation

Score percentage = (correct answers ÷ total questions) × 100.

Example: 42 correct answers out of 50 questions gives 84%.

Practice scores are indicators of preparation, not guarantees of the official examination result. Ensure that practice questions cover the current blueprint and are of appropriate difficulty.

## 8. Study Plan

### Phase 1: Foundations

* [ ] Learn Business Analysis terminology.
* [ ] Understand needs, changes, solutions, stakeholders, value, and context.
* [ ] Distinguish business, stakeholder, solution, and transition requirements.
* [ ] Review the current exam blueprint.

### Phase 2: Core Techniques

* [ ] Practice stakeholder analysis.
* [ ] Study elicitation and collaboration techniques.
* [ ] Practice requirement verification and validation.
* [ ] Review traceability and prioritization.
* [ ] Practice process models and scenario analysis.

### Phase 3: Application

* [ ] Complete short topic-based quizzes.
* [ ] Analyze business scenarios.
* [ ] Explain why incorrect answer choices are unsuitable.
* [ ] Record recurring mistakes.
* [ ] Revisit weak topics.

### Phase 4: Exam Preparation

* [ ] Complete timed mock exams.
* [ ] Review all incorrect and guessed answers.
* [ ] Confirm exam logistics and current policies.
* [ ] Prepare a concise final revision sheet.
* [ ] Avoid relying exclusively on memorized answer patterns.

## 9. Completion Criteria

Consider a topic ready for final review when you can:

* Explain its purpose in your own words.
* Define its key terms.
* Identify when and why it is used.
* Apply it to a realistic business scenario.
* Distinguish it from similar concepts.
* Answer practice questions and explain your reasoning.
* Identify any remaining uncertainty.
