# IIBA ECBA Certification Study Repository

## Overview

This repository contains structured Markdown study materials to support preparation for the **IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA)**.

The repository is designed for self-study, concept revision, practical application, practice questions, mock exams, application tracking, and progress monitoring.

**Important:** This is an independent study repository. Its modules, practice questions, and trackers are learning resources, not official IIBA publications or exam questions. Always use the current IIBA ECBA exam blueprint and official policies as the source of truth.

## Recommended Study Workflow

### Phase 1: Learn

* Read the relevant module.
* Identify the key terminology.
* Write a concise summary in your own words.
* Create examples from realistic business situations.
* Compare concepts that are easy to confuse.

### Phase 2: Apply

* Practice classifying requirements.
* Analyze stakeholder needs and conflicts.
* Practice elicitation and collaboration scenarios.
* Interpret business process models.
* Evaluate solutions against business needs and expected value.

### Phase 3: Test

* Complete topic-based practice questions.
* Attempt mock exams under timed conditions.
* Explain why the correct answer is appropriate.
* Record incorrect answers and uncertain guesses.
* Revisit weak areas before attempting another test.

### Phase 4: Prepare for the Examination

* Check the latest official ECBA exam blueprint.
* Confirm current eligibility and registration requirements.
* Review examination policies and available delivery options.
* Verify fees directly with IIBA.
* Schedule the examination when your preparation and budget allow.
* Complete final revision and confirm examination logistics.

## Suggested Weekly Routine

A flexible routine for a candidate studying alongside other commitments:

* **Session 1:** Learn a new topic.
* **Session 2:** Review terminology and create examples.
* **Session 3:** Practice questions and scenario analysis.
* **Session 4:** Review mistakes and revise weak areas.
* **Weekly review:** Update progress, practice scores, and the next week's plan.

Adjust the number and length of sessions to your available study time.

## Quality Standards for Study Notes

Each learning module should ideally include:

* Learning objectives.
* Definitions and key terminology.
* Explanations of core concepts.
* Practical business examples.
* Techniques and when to use them.
* Common mistakes and exam traps.
* Scenario-based questions.
* Answer explanations.
* Revision checklist.
* Reliable learning resources.
